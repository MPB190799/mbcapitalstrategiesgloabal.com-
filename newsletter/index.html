§06 · Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

How does the 30-day trial work?

You start with full access immediately. Stripe charges nothing during the first 30 days. If you cancel before the trial ends, it's completely free. After that, monthly billing starts automatically — cancellable anytime.

Sector plan: do I only get stocks from Marco's portfolio?

No. You get the complete sector universe by market capitalization. So you don't miss any important industry story, even if Marco doesn't hold that stock himself.

What's the difference between the €8 and €5 plan?

The €8 plan is Marcos personal stock picks (portfolio + watchlist), cross-sector. You get every earnings report from his ~227 hard asset and dividend positions — across all sectors. The €5 plan focuses on a single sector with the complete industry universe. Both plans deliver identical analysis depth (charts, KPIs, CEO quotes, risks, opportunities, neutral assessment).

Sector plan: do I get emails about other sectors?

No, exclusively your chosen sector(s). Nobody gets spammed with irrelevant topics.

Goody plan: can I hide individual sectors?

Yes. At checkout (or later in the customer portal) you can opt out of individual sectors — e.g. "Steel" or "Utilities" — if Marco's picks there don't interest you.

How do I cancel? (1-click, no hassle)

Every premium email includes a "Manage / Cancel Subscription" link at the bottom. Click leads directly to the Stripe Customer Portal — there you see your subscription and can cancel with one click. Your access runs until the end of your already paid period (e.g. purchased on the 25th → access until the 25th of next month), then no more charges — no remaining term trap, no minimum commitment. Instantly confirmed. No email to support, no hotline, no appointment needed.

Is this financial advice?

No. Marco's newsletters are educational content and market context — not individual advice, no buy/sell recommendations, no guarantee of future price movements.
