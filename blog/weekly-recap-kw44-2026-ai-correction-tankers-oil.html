Weekly Recap KW44 (Oct 26 – Nov 1, 2026): AI Correction, Burning Tankers — and Oil Falls Anyway

The Number of the Week: A Good Three Percentage Points — DAX vs S&P 500

The DAX gained 2.53% in KW44, closing at 25,818 points. The S&P 500 gave up 0.57% over the same period, landing at 7,580 points. That is a gap of a good three percentage points — in a single week.

The reason is no mystery: in the US, exactly what had carried the market for months corrected. Communication services (XLC) lost 5.32%, technology (XLK) 2.03% — the AI heavyweights declined, while Europe with its value and industrial weighting benefited. Assessment, not fact: weeks like this show how much AI expectation is packed into US indices — and how little of it sits in the DAX.

Macro: Fed and ECB Both Pause

Both major central banks were up this week — and both stood still. The Fed decision carried a 10:2 vote; the ECB also held rates steady on Thursday (Oct 29) — the deposit rate stays at 2.50%, last moved in September. So this week, both major central banks paused.

Ten-year US Treasuries sit at 4.93%, the VIX at 19.42 — remarkably calm for a week with an AI correction and burning tankers.

Oil: Four Tankers Ablaze — and Brent Falls Anyway

Between Tuesday (Oct 27) and Friday (Oct 30), four tankers were attacked and set ablaze at the Strait of Hormuz. In the past, such a headline sequence would have driven oil prices up by double digits. This week: Brent fell 3.76% to $112.33.

The mechanics behind it: the market has apparently priced the attacks as regionally contained, as long as the actual flow through the strait continues. The Brent-WTI spread stands at $9.38 — the risk premium has not vanished, it is simply no longer growing. Assessment, not fact: anyone buying oil purely on the fear headline pays the price in weeks like this. What matters is what actually gets through the strait.

Gold: Year-to-Date Low in the Middle of a Crisis Week

Gold lost 3.07%, falling to $3,884.90 — a year-to-date low. Crisis week, burning tankers, and the classic safe haven corrects anyway after a long rally. Market history, not a forecast: even gold knows profit-taking.

Sector Rotation: Healthcare over AI

The eleven US sector ETFs show the week's direction clearly:

  • Winners: Health care (XLV) +3.12%, materials (XLB) +2.79%, utilities (XLU) +2.04%, consumer staples (XLP) +1.31%, industrials (XLI) +1.69%
  • Losers: Communication services (XLC) −5.32%, technology (XLK) −2.03%

This is a reading of sector rotation based on the eleven sector ETFs, not a separately measured flow statistic. Defensive names led, the AI heavyweights corrected — exactly the pattern that explains the DAX outperformance.

Among tankers: Frontline +7.5%, TORM +7.0% — in the middle of the Hormuz week. Helmerich & Payne (+10.4%), Santander (+7.6%), UPS (+7.1%) and Dow (+6.4%) also gained strongly.

On the other side: TGS −13.0%, VOC Energy −12.0%, Vale Indonesia −10.0%, Petrobras −8.0%, plus Glencore (−3.4%), Fresnillo (−3.2%) and Newmont (−2.1%) — the miners were caught in the gold pullback.

The Bottom Line

KW44 was a week of reversals: AI names correct, Europe beats the US, oil falls despite burning tankers, gold falls despite the crisis. For a cashflow portfolio, little changes — distributions kept flowing, charter contracts kept running, and the rotation toward value if anything favors the approach.
