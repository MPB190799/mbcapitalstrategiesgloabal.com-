Weekly Recap KW40: FOMC Hike Ripples Through Hard Assets

As of October 2, 2026 · Not investment advice.

The Week in Brief

Week 40 (September 28 – October 3, 2026) was dominated by one theme: The Fed raised interest rates by 25 basis points to 5.25–5.50% on September 25. Powell continued signaling "higher for longer" — and the market is responding.

For hard asset investors, this means: borrowing costs are rising, but cash flow remains king. Companies generating revenue today are better positioned than those promising profitability in some distant future.

FOMC Hike — Sector Impact

FACT: The Fed raised rates by +25 bp to 5.25–5.50% on September 25, 2026 (Source: FOMC Statement Sept 25). The dollar strengthened in the aftermath.

Upstream Oil

Brent crude remains stable in the $70–75/bbl range. A stronger USD exerts mild downward pressure on the price, but fundamental demand — particularly from Asia and chokepoint risks like Hormuz — provides a cushion. Positions like Cline Resources (CLCO) showed +17% in September.

REITs

H higher rates mean higher discount rates and therefore more pressure on REIT valuations. STAG Industrial gave up slightly (~−5%) in September. This is classic rate sensitivity — not structurally concerning as long as rental income remains stable.

Silver Mining

Gold is under pressure (~$3,280/oz post-hike), which spills over to silver miners like Perimeter Solutions (PSEC). On the day after the FOMC decision, there was a −5% drop — short-term reaction, not a trend break.

Tesla / EV

Momentum-driven and largely rate-immune: +36% in September. Valuation is extremely high, which increases risk — but the market rewards narrative over fundamentals here.

Portfolio Impact in Review

Nine additional purchases were made in September — a mix of "buy when others sell" and selective DCA. Performance varies widely:

  • CLCO (Upstream): +17% in September, dividend $0.26/quarter stable
  • TSLA (EV/Momentum): +36%, but no dividend — purely capital appreciation play
  • PSEC (Silver Mining): +8%, under gold pressure post-FOMC
  • STAG (REIT): −5% in September, rate sensitivity showing through

Outlook for Week 41

The Fed has communicated its line clearly. In week 41, watch for:

  • Inflation data (CPI/PCE) — do they confirm the "higher for longer" thesis?
  • Brent development — does the $70–75 corridor hold or do chokepoint tensions tip the scale?
  • USD strength — a continued strong dollar pressures emerging markets and commodity prices in USD terms.

My Take: Hard assets with cash flow today are better positioned than growth stories that only make money tomorrow. The interest rate is real — those who ignore it will pay the price.
