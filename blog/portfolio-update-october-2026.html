How did my dividend portfolio do in October 2026? Dividends came to €405.13 gross and €363.49 net, and performance was 4% (S&P 500 +1.21%, DAX +3.23%). Sanofi and OR Royalties are new in the portfolio. I sold nothing.

Portfolio Update October 2026: €363 in Dividends and Two New Positions

Published: 23.11.2026  ·  Data as of: October 2026; add-on purchases and shipping dividends as of 6 Nov 2026  ·  Note: This article is not investment advice. It is for information and personal assessment only. No recommendation to buy or sell.

📌 In short: €363 in net dividends, 4% portfolio performance and two new positions. The largest payers were CMB.TECH and Thungela Resources. I expect about €186 in November and about €764 in December. My personal dividend yield is 5.61%.

The video for this article

1. Dividends in October: €405 gross, €363 net

October brought €405.13 gross and €363.49 net, with about €40 withheld in tax. The largest payers:

  • CMB.TECH: about €100 net, my largest position
  • Thungela Resources: about €82, paid in October because of the longer payment period
  • Central Asia Metals: €34
  • Exxaro Resources: €15
  • Medical Property Trust: €13

Further payments came from Innovative Industrial Properties, Horizon Oil, BCE, Rhythm Capital, COSCO Shipping, PTT, Realty Income, Acadian Timber, Bank of Nova Scotia, Philip Morris, Trinity Industries, Oxford Lane, Main Street Capital, Baxter and Itaú Unibanco.

2. Buys and sells

New in the portfolio: Sanofi (7 shares at €71.44, about €500 invested) and OR Royalties (13 shares at €30.86). Sanofi's dividend yield at purchase was about 4.9%, with dividend growth of 3.1% a year over ten years and 4.47% over five.

Added on 6 Nov: Medical Property Trust (90 shares) and Central Asia Metals (90 shares, about €1.60).

Sells: none in October.

3. Winners and losers

Winners: Sanofi (+11%), Chevron, Philip Morris, Pfizer, PayPal, Devon Energy, British American Tobacco, ONEOK, Whitecap Resources, Thungela.

Losers: MARA Holdings, Champion Iron, Medical Property Trust, Oxford Lane, Banco do Brasil, Rio Tinto, B2Gold, Central Asia Metals, ING.

I show both sides, including the positions in the red. A portfolio update without losers would not be an honest account.

4. How the portfolio is spread

North America and Europe lead, followed by more than 10% Asia, 7% South America, 5.2% Africa and 2.92% Oceania. By company size: 1.34% mega caps, 41% large caps, about 26% mid caps, a similar share of small caps and 0.96% micro caps.

5. Shipping dividends in 2026

Net dividends from shipping in 2026, as of 6 Nov: Torm €273, CMB.TECH €234, BW LPG €190, International Seaways €159, Frontline €124. Since purchase, Dorian LPG has paid €557, Flex LNG €500 and Torm €418.

6. My key figures and the outlook

  • Personal dividend yield: 5.61%
  • Average dividends per month: €393
  • Dividends over the last twelve months: €4,718
  • Dividends in total: €9,353

For November I expect about €186, including payments from AT&T, British American Tobacco, Realty Income, Petrobras, Equinor, Genco Shipping, Dorian LPG, Hercules Capital, Arbor Realty Trust, DHT Holdings, APA and Serica Energy. For December I expect about €764.

My goal remains €1,000 in net dividends per month by 2030.
