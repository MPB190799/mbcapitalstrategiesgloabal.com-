Our Analysis Methodology

By Marco Bozem | Independent Investor

Fundamental Principles of Our Work

MB Capital Strategies is built on three fundamental principles:

  1. Primary Sources: We analyze exclusively original documents — annual reports, SEC filings, company presentations and directly published financial data.
  2. No Investment Advice: All content serves information purposes. Every analysis is clearly labeled as independent research, not investment recommendations.
  3. Transparent Calculations: Our yield calculations (dividend yields, YOC, DRIP) are based on official company data and publicly available sources. We document our methodology openly.

Our Research Process

We follow a disciplined approach to investment research:

  • Data Collection: We gather information from official company filings (10-K, 10-Q for US companies), annual reports, and regulatory submissions.
  • Cross-Verification: Data points are cross-checked across multiple sources to ensure accuracy and reliability.
  • Independent Analysis: We perform our own calculations and assessments without relying solely on third-party ratings or analyst opinions.
  • Primary Source Focus: Our analysis prioritizes original documents over secondary commentary, ensuring we understand the full context of each investment opportunity.

What We Cover

Our research focuses on hard-asset sectors with strong cash flow characteristics:

  • Shipping & Maritime: Dry bulk, tanker, and LNG carriers — analyzing fleet capacity, freight rates, and charter terms.
  • Mining & Metals: Copper, gold, lithium, and other industrial metals — evaluating reserves, production costs, and geopolitical exposure.
  • Energy Infrastructure: Pipelines, midstream assets, and REITs — assessing cash flow stability and dividend coverage ratios.
  • Industrial Assets: Companies with tangible assets and consistent dividend histories.

Our Standards

We maintain rigorous standards in our research:

  • Source Transparency: We cite our sources and link to original documents whenever possible.
  • Clear Disclaimers: Every analysis includes appropriate disclaimers about investment risks and the non-advisory nature of our content.
  • Regular Updates: Our research is updated as new data becomes available, with clear versioning and modification dates.
  • E-E-A-T Compliance: We demonstrate Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness through transparent methodology and verifiable sources.
